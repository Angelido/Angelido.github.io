Hi, I'm Angelo — a PhD student in **Computer Science** at the University of Pisa. I hold a Bachelor's degree in **Mathematics** from UniPi, followed by a Master's degree in **Computer Science**, also at Pisa, with a specialization in Artificial Intelligence. 

My field is **algorithms and data structures**, with a primary focus on **lossless text compression**. By "text" I mean any string of symbols — from DNA sequences to structured data — but my interest centers in particular on texts with a grammatical structure, namely natural language and source code.

In my work I explore classical theoretical techniques, but I am also interested in methods that integrate AI models in a deterministic fashion. Compression is the main theme of my current research, though my broader interest extends to the field of algorithms and data structures more generally. In the past I have also worked on AI applications in **medical contexts**, an experience that broadened my perspective on what it means to do research with a concrete impact.
